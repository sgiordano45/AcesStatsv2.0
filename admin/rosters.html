Rosters

Each season's rosters are what Enter stats, the Game Tracker, lineups and the team pages use. Changes here are held until you save.

Known players keep their ID and account. Someone new gets an ID made from their name.

A file with playerName and team columns puts each player on that team for the season, moving them off any other team. Nothing is saved until you press Save changes.

Copies the teams from the offseason roster board into this season. Players who stay keep their number, position, captain flag and linked account.

Looks through this season's saved rosters for unlinked accounts, wrong player IDs, players on two teams, and numbers, positions or bats/throws that differ from the player's profile. Fixes save right away.

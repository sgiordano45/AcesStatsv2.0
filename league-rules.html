League Rules

Mountainside Aces official regulations for the 2026 Summer and Fall seasons.

Official documents

Permits, memos and other documents from the Recreation Department.

Rosters and eligibility

Summer eligibility

Residency

Must live or work in Mountainside.

Out-of-town players

Teams may roster at most 3 out-of-town players.

  • Players who work for Mountainside (Police, DPW, Boro Admin) count as residents.

Roster size

Capped at 15 players for the summer season.

  • More players may be added only if the other teams are full.

Fall eligibility

Players must meet one of these:

  • Live or work in Mountainside and be over 30 years old.
  • Have played in a previous Fall season ('22, '23, '24).

Free agency draft pool

Players can no longer switch teams freely. At the end of every season, returning players either:

  • Stay on their current team, or
  • Enter the free agency draft pool, drafted in reverse order of last season's standings (regular season and playoff records).

Exempt from the pool

  • Immediate family (father, son or brother).
  • Out-of-town players.

Competitive rebalancing

  • Rosters may be combined or adjusted from season to season to improve balance.
  • A minimum number of players on each roster will be enforced.
  • Final roster sizes depend on registration totals and the number of teams.

General rules

Regular season

Summer

23 games per team, then double-elimination playoffs.

Fall

12 games per team, then double-elimination playoffs.

Players needed to start

Summer: 8 rostered players

  • Fewer than 8 is an automatic forfeit (the other team wins 7-0).
  • If neither team has 8, the game is recorded as a tie.

Fall: 7 rostered players

  • Fewer than 7 is an automatic forfeit (the other team wins 7-0).
  • If neither team has 7, the game is recorded as a tie.

Game time and lineups

Time limit

No new inning starts after 60 minutes of play.

  • Game 1's clock starts at the scheduled time, no exceptions.
  • Taking the field late is lost time.
  • No time limit in the playoffs.

Official game

5 full innings (4½ if the home team is leading).

  • An incomplete game restarts on the makeup date.

Adding players mid-game

Rostered players can join before the start of the 4th inning.

  • New players bat at the bottom of the order.
  • Nobody may join after the first pitch of the 4th inning.

8 players

With only 8 rostered players, the other team provides a catcher.

  • That catcher can't make any plays in the field.
  • Other fielders cover home plate.

9 players

With 9 players, one must catch. No courtesy catcher, no exceptions.

Conduct

Ejections

  • 1st: out for the rest of the game, plus a 1-game suspension.
  • 2nd: 3-game suspension.
  • 3rd: suspended for the rest of the season.

Fighting

Any player who strikes another or is involved in a fight is thrown out of the league indefinitely.

Spikes

No metal spikes.

Summer substitutes

No substitutes

There are no substitutes in the Summer season, no exceptions.

Fall substitutes

When a sub is allowed

Only when a team has 7 of its own players available.

  • The sub brings the team to 8 so the game can be played.
  • The other team provides a courtesy catcher.

Lineup and position

The sub bats last and plays right field.

One sub per player

A player may sub only once in the whole season.

  • After subbing once, a player can't sub again for anyone.

In-game rules

Lineup and defense

Batting

Every player who shows up must be in that game's batting order.

Defense

Players don't have to play the field. Defense is entirely the team's decision.

Pitching

Arc

Slow pitch, 6 to 12 feet: no lower, no higher.

Pitcher's position

One foot on the rubber while throwing.

  • Pitching from behind the rubber, one foot must be in line with it.

Pitcher's equipment

Nothing on the throwing hand.

Strike zone

Plate extension

A rubber extension is used for calling strikes.

  • A pitch that lands on home plate or the extension is a strike.

Base running

Leaving the base

Runners wait until contact.

  • Leaving early can be called out by the umpire.

Scoring

Runners may touch home plate or the extension to score.

Non-contact league

Slide or avoid contact ("give yourself up") at all times.

  • Applies to double-play attempts.
  • Hard take-out slides and baseline interference are an automatic double play.

Phantom tags

Not tolerated. Penalty at the umpire's discretion.

Batting

Count

Every batter starts 1-1.

  • A foul with 2 strikes is a strikeout.
  • No courtesy foul.

Leaving early

If a player has to leave early, the order skips his spot. No automatic out.

Batter's box

At least one foot stays in the box through contact.

  • Stepping out with both feet is an automatic out.

No distractions

No yelling, shouting or noise from the pitch through the swing.

  • Yelling during a pitch is an automatic ball, even if the play was an out.
  • If the result was a hit, the hit stands.
  • The player gets a warning; a second warning is an ejection.

Mercy rule

Game ends at

  • 15 runs after 4 innings.
  • 12 runs after 5 innings.

Field boundaries

Light poles and boxes

  • Above the fence line: home run.
  • Below the fence line: live ball.

Deerfield ground rule

A ball through the center-field gap is a ground-rule triple.

Catch and carry

If a fielder catches a ball in play and momentum carries him out of play:

  • The ball stays live.
  • Runners are not awarded a base.

Ball thrown out of play

The play is dead, and:

  • Thrown from the infield: runners get 1 base.
  • Thrown from the outfield: runners get 2 bases.

Disputes

When something isn't covered by these rules, or there's a dispute:

  • Only captains discuss rule interpretations or raise issues with the umpire.
  • Judgment calls are not argued by anybody.
  • Without agreement, the umpire decides.

Courtesy runners

Before the game

A team may name up to 2 players who get a courtesy runner for the whole game.

Injuries

More courtesy runners are allowed for players hurt during the game.

  • Once an injured player takes a courtesy runner, he uses one from then on.
  • No limit per inning for injuries.

Who runs

The last batted out.

  • No outs yet this inning: the last batted out from the previous inning.
  • A second runner needed before another out: the second-to-last batted out.
  • No outs at all yet in the first inning: the last player in the batting order.
  • The same runner can't run twice in a row.

Scheduling

Schedule lock

The whole regular season and playoff schedule is set and locked before the season starts.

Weather

Games are rescheduled only for weather.

  • Rainout dates are built into the schedule.
  • Makeups go on days that already have games.

A team can't field enough players

The other captain can either:

  • Take the forfeit win, or
  • Reschedule with the other captain.

Neither team can field enough players

The game is a tie, or rescheduled if both captains agree.

Playoff start date

The playoffs start on a fixed date.

  • Summer: teams that haven't played all 23 games are seeded by winning percentage.
  • Fall: teams that haven't played all 12 games are seeded by winning percentage.

Playoffs

Games needed to be eligible

  • Summer: play in at least 8 of your team's regular-season games.
  • Fall: play in at least 4 of your team's regular-season games.

Seeding tiebreakers

  • 2 teams tied: head-to-head, then runs allowed, then run differential.
  • 3 or more teams tied: runs allowed, then run differential.

Equipment

Bats

Wood bats only.

Softballs

Clinchers, supplied by the league.

How It Works

  • Tap a team to pick it. Tap it again to clear the pick. Picks save automatically.
  • Each game locks at its scheduled first pitch, so you can make or change Thursday's picks on Wednesday.
  • Nobody can see anyone else's pick for a game until it locks.
  • 1 point for every correct pick.
  • +1 upset bonus when you're right and fewer than a third of pickers took that team (at least 3 picks on the game).
  • Ties, forfeits, and cancelled games don't count for anyone.
  • Season standings: points, then correct picks, then fewest misses.

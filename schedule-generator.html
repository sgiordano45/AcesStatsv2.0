📅 Season Structure

Opening Week: OD Sunday (6 games) + Mon–Thu 4 DHs (OD DH team + 2 others sit).
Weeks 2–11: Sun–Thu, 5 DHs per week, 1 team on bye.
Week 12: Sun 1 DH + Mon–Thu 8 singles (2/night).
Week 13: Sun 3 singles.
Totals: 22 games per team from DHs/singles + OD = 23 each (OD DH team = 24).

Fall League — Fixed Date Schedule (Sep 13 – Dec 6)
  • 10 Sundays: Sep 13, 20, 27 · Oct 4, 11, 18, 25 · Nov 1, 8, 15
  • 10 teams: 65 regular season games (13 per team) — Sundays filled first, then weeknights
  • 11 teams: 72 regular season games (13/team avg) — all 11 teams play every Sunday, one team plays a DH (6 games/Sunday, 8am–1pm)
  • H2H: Every team plays each other at least 1x, no more than 2x
  • Playoffs: Double elimination after regular season, championship Dec 6
